# Theme Art Attribution

The seasonal theme illustrations in this directory are **Twemoji** graphics.

- Source: [jdecked/twemoji](https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji) (maintained fork of Twitter's Twemoji)
- License: [CC-BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

They are used as decorative theme art in the Iris admin theme picker and as the
faint page-background tiles behind the seasonal site themes (`halloween`,
`christmas`). The SVGs are copied verbatim from the Twemoji asset set.
